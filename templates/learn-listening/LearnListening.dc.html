Questions learners ask

What level is this episode for?+

A1, absolute beginner. Short sentences, everyday market and time vocabulary.

Is this Mexican or Spain Spanish?+

Mexican, recorded by a teacher from Guadalajara. The grammar is the same everywhere.

How long until I understand fast Spanish?+

Most learners follow normal-speed Spanish on familiar topics after 80–150 hours of focused listening.

Should I read the English?+

On pass 2 only. Switch to "ES only" once you know the story.
